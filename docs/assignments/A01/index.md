Portfolio 1 - [Riley Shaffer](https://uncc.instructure.com/eportfolios/3698/Home/About_Me)

Within each assignment tab there are descriptions, photos, and downloadable links that further go into detail about each assignment and its functions. Overall very well organized and simple. B. Reproducibility: Taking a deeper dive into Shaffers' each unique assignment tab to dictate whether these designs would be able to be reproduced given his information. In my personal opinion Shaffer provides an abundant amount of information including hand drawn equations, CAD designs, calculations, schematics, and explanations. Free body diagrams and thorough findings are supported by step-by-step instructions so that anyone who possesses an engineering background could easily digest and reproduce. I do believe that this prompts the user to recreate and understand his work. 

C. Evidence of reasoning: Shaffer does an excellent job in this section of the criteria. His technical calculations and findings are always supported by real descriptions of the thought process used to achieve the answer. Shaffer includes personal struggles, collaborations, and breakthroughs in a short description before presenting any information to the user, this also goes to continue while presenting information using his references to support each photo to allow the user to gain a more broad understanding and scope of the project. Through this technique the user is able to better gauge the challenges that could occur when attempting to recreate. Creating a more authentic experience. 

D. Professional Tone: After analyzing and reviewing Shaffers' portfolio I do believe that he used adequate and technical verbiage in his descriptions. As I would assume that this was held paramount in a fluid portfolio such as this. Words such as "sectional area", "deflecting equation", and "mounting shaft" just to name a few. Highlight the scope of the topics discussed throughout this portfolio. A very skilled engineer that is held up to the standards of an employer.
